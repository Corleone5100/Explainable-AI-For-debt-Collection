5. LEARNING PHASE ANALYSIS
================================================================================

PHASE 1: EXPLORATION (Episodes 1-50, ~5,000 timesteps)
  Smoothed reward: Rs. -751K to -846K
  Behavior: Agent tries random actions, applies Legal Notice too aggressively
  on Low risk borrowers, incurs massive penalties
  Key issue: Policy has not yet learned to differentiate risk categories

PHASE 2: TRANSITION (Episodes 50-200, ~5,000-20,000 timesteps)
  Smoothed reward: Crosses from negative to positive (~episode 100-150)
  Behavior: Agent learns the basic risk-action mapping (High->Legal, Low->SMS)
  The gradient from the reward function starts overriding initial randomness

PHASE 3: RAPID IMPROVEMENT (Episodes 200-500, ~20,000-50,000 timesteps)
  Smoothed reward: Jumps from Rs. 0 to Rs. 230K+
  Behavior: Agent refines its policy, learns to handle Very High risk
  borrowers effectively. The GAT embeddings start contributing meaningfully
  to decision quality

PHASE 4: PLATEAU (Episodes 500-1003, ~50,000-100,000 timesteps)
  Smoothed reward: Gradual climb from Rs. 230K to Rs. 260K
  Behavior: Policy has largely converged. The agent is fine-tuning its
  handling of Medium risk borrowers (the hardest category). Growth slows
  significantly — diminishing returns from additional training

7. WHAT NEEDS IMPROVEMENT
================================================================================

ISSUE 1: SLOW INITIAL LEARNING (Episodes 1-50)
  Problem: First 50 episodes average Rs. -751K to -846K smoothed reward
  Impact: Wasted training compute; the agent takes 5,000+ timesteps just
  to escape the negative regime
  Root cause: The reward function's penalties are so large (Legal on Low
  risk = Rs. 800 + 35% of total_demand penalty) that the agent needs
  many episodes to discover the correct action mapping
  Fix: Pre-train with behavioral cloning from the rule-based policy for
  5,000-10,000 timesteps, then switch to PPO. This gives the agent a
  reasonable starting point instead of random exploration.
  Expected: Reduce Q1 negative from -62K to +50K, save ~20,000 timesteps

ISSUE 2: HIGH EPISODE-TO-EPISODE VARIANCE
  Problem: Raw reward standard deviation is ~Rs. 200K+, range spans Rs. 2M
  Impact: Noisy gradient updates, slow convergence
  Root cause: The agent's reward depends heavily on which borrowers it
  encounters in each episode. An episode with 30 Very High risk borrowers
  yields Rs. 800K+; one with 30 Low risk yields -Rs. 1.1M
  Fix: Increase VecNormalize's clip_obs and clip_reward parameters.
  Currently clip_obs=10.0. Also consider reward clipping in PPO
  (clip_range parameter)
  Expected: Smoother learning curves, faster convergence

ISSUE 3: PLATEAU AT SUBOPTIMAL LEVEL
  Problem: All seeds plateau around Rs. 250K-270K, well below the theoretical
  maximum. The rule-based policy achieves Rs. 3,671 per decision vs PPO's
  Rs. 2,513.
  Impact: The PPO agent is 46% less efficient than a simple heuristic
  Root cause: The over-escalation problem on Low/Medium risk borrowers
  (detailed in evaluation_report.txt). The agent hasn't learned to restrain
  itself on easy borrowers
  Fix: The same fix as in the evaluation report — increase penalties for
  Legal on Low risk from 0.35x to 0.6x of total_demand
  Expected: +Rs. 1.5M improvement, bringing PPO to Rs. 3,000+/decision

ISSUE 4: SEED SENSITIVITY IN LEARNING SPEED
  Problem: Seed 42 grows 271.7% while Seed 43 grows only 183.9% — a 48%
  difference in learning rate
  Impact: Single-seed evaluations may not be representative
  Fix: Use a higher learning rate warmup period, or increase n_steps from
  512 to 2048 in PPO to reduce policy update variance
  Expected: More consistent learning across seeds (CV < 5%)

ISSUE 5: INSUFFICIENT TRAINING TIMESTEPS FOR FULL FEATURE SET
  Problem: The ablation study shows the full 150-feature model fails at
  100K timesteps (-Rs. 15.4M), but even the optimal 16-feature model
  hasn't fully converged (still climbing at episode 1003)
  Impact: The agent may be 10-15% below its true potential
  Fix: Increase training to 200K-500K timesteps for the full feature model
  Expected: An additional 10-20% improvement in final reward

ISSUE 6: NO LEARNING CURVE FOR EVALUATION MODEL
  Problem: The production model (graph_rl_debt_model.zip) was trained for
  500K timesteps, but we only have learning curves for 100K timesteps
  (ablation runs). We don't know if the 500K model continued improving
  or plateaued
  Fix: Run learning_curves.py with RL_TOTAL_TIMESTEPS=500000
  Expected: Confirm whether 500K timesteps is sufficient or if more is needed

8. RECOMMENDATIONS (PRIORITIZED)
================================================================================

  PRIORITY 1: Behavioral Cloning Warmup (HIGH IMPACT, LOW EFFORT)
    Pre-train with rule-based actions for 10K timesteps before PPO
    Expected: Eliminate the negative Q1 entirely, reduce training time by 20%

  PRIORITY 2: Increase Legal-on-Low Penalty (HIGH IMPACT, MEDIUM EFFORT)
    Change debt_env.py penalty from 0.35 to 0.6 for Legal on Low risk
    Retrain and re-evaluate
    Expected: +Rs. 1.5M improvement, PPO surpasses rule-based efficiency

  PRIORITY 3: Increase Training to 200K+ for Full Model (MEDIUM IMPACT)
    The learning curves are still climbing at 100K timesteps
    Expected: Additional 10-20% improvement, better convergence

  PRIORITY 4: Reward Clipping and Normalization (MEDIUM IMPACT)
    Clip rewards to [-1000, +1000] range to reduce gradient variance
    Expected: Smoother learning, fewer catastrophic episodes

  PRIORITY 5: Multi-Seed Production Evaluation (LOW IMPACT, VALIDATION)
    Train the final model with 5 seeds instead of 1, select the best
    Expected: More robust production model, reduced variance

================================================================================
9. CONCLUSION
================================================================================

The learning curves demonstrate a healthy training process:
  - Clear learning signal (not random noise)
  - Consistent convergence across 3 seeds (CV = 10.4%)
  - Minimal train-eval gap (2.7% = good generalization)
  - 89.5% positive episodes

However, the agent plateaus at a suboptimal level — 46% below the rule-based
baseline in per-decision efficiency. The root cause is consistent across
evaluation and learning curves: over-escalation on Low/Medium risk borrowers.

The learning curves also reveal that 100K timesteps is insufficient for full
convergence. The agent is still improving at episode 1003, suggesting that
200K-500K timesteps would yield meaningful gains.

Most critically, the first 50 episodes are wasted on catastrophic exploration.
A behavioral cloning warmup from the rule-based policy would eliminate this
waste and accelerate training significantly.
